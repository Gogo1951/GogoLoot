# GogoLoot

Speedy auto loot empties corpses without the loot window, auto rolls Need, Greed, or Pass by quality, and master looting hands out drops for you. Clams, containers, and unlocked lockboxes open on their own. Loot faster, pull sooner.

**TL;DR:** You can't click corpses and pull the next pack at the same time. Don't let loot slow down your zug!

### Speedy Loot

* **Corpses Empty Instantly** // Everything lands in your bags the moment you open a corpse, with no loot window in the way.
* **Peek When You Want** // Hold Shift as you loot to see the window like always.
* **Hear the Good Stuff** // A chime plays when something worth a look drops, so it never slips by unseen.
* **Your Call** // Switch it off and looting works the way it always has.

### Automated Rolls

<img width="800" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bd1ca322-a1b4-4e1e-b325-91d2cfbb4eb0" />

* **Gear You Want Waits for You** // Tick the stats, armor, and weapons each character uses, and matching gear is left for you to roll.
* **The Rest Rolls Itself** // Everything else gets Need, Greed, or Pass up to the quality you pick, set apart for parties and raids.
* **Any Item, Its Own Roll** // Give a specific item a roll of its own, quest items included.
* **Safety First** // Never rolls on recipes, books, mounts, pets, or legendaries, or on BoP gear unless you say so, and warns you when you zone in set to Need or Pass.
* **Ready Out of the Box** // Starts on, greeding Uncommon and lower. Change any of it, or switch it off.

### Automated Opening

<img width="800" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8a232a0d-ad85-4108-b4dd-a04f4afd49b0" />

* **Bags Open Themselves** // Clams, crates, coin purses, and unlocked lockboxes open while you keep playing.
* **Lockboxes Sorted** // Lockbox tooltips show the Lockpicking skill each one needs, and the mini-map menu lists any still waiting on a Rogue.
* **Safety First** // Raid boss containers stay sealed so you can trade or sell them, and nothing opens in combat.
* **Your List** // Set any container to Open or Ignore, or switch it off entirely.

### Automated Master Looting

* **Drops Go Where You Say** // Pick who gets each quality, and every drop is handed out the moment you open the loot window.
* **The Raid Stays in the Loop** // Your group hears who holds each quality and who got what, even items you hand out yourself.
* **Set Up in Seconds** // Quick Settings pops up the moment you become Master Looter.
* **Safety First** // Recipes, books, mounts, pets, legendaries, and anything on your Ignore List wait for you to hand out.
* **Off Until You Want It** // Starts off. Turn it on when you're leading the raid.

> Sample Messages

> **Pick Who Holds Loot, Raid:** Aero will be holding Uncommon items for the group // GogoLoot

### Loot Toasts

* **See Every Drop** // Each item and coin you loot pops up on screen, so nothing slips past while Speedy Loot skips the window.
* **Your Group's Loot Too** // See what your party or raid picks up, who looted it, and the roll that won it.
* **Show What Matters** // Pick which loot gets a toast, by type and quality, for you and your group.
* **Off Until You Want It** // New and off to start. Turn it on and tell us what you think!

## Setup

1. Install the add-on, ideally using [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/gogoloot) or [Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/gogoloot).
2. Type `/gogo`, or pick GogoLoot Options in the mini-map menu, to open the Options Interface.
3. Automated Rolls is already on. Tick what each character wants under Character Overrides.
4. Raid leading? Turn on Automated Master Looting and pick who gets each quality.
5. _"If you ain't first, you're last."_

## Tips & Tricks

### Mini-map Button

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Hover | Opens the mini-map menu |
| Left-Click | Toggles Automated Rolls |

The menu switches each feature on or off, shows what Automated Rolls rolls in a party and in a raid, and lists any lockboxes still waiting on a Rogue.

### Game Settings

Speedy Loot and Automated Opening need the game's Auto Loot setting, so GogoLoot turns it on while either one is on. While Loot Toasts is on, your General chat tab's Item Loot and Money Loot lines step aside, and they come back when you turn the toasts off.

### Good to Know

* **Trade Announcements** // Every completed trade gets a summary of items, enchants, and gold, whispered to your partner or posted to the group.
* **Tradable Gear Tint** // BoP gear you can still trade is tinted in your bags, so you see what's yours to hand out before it binds.
* **Roll Macro** // One button rolls 1-100, 1-99 for off-spec, or a raid roll, and GogoLoot names whoever a raid roll lands on.
* **Quiet Roll Chat** // The game's roll spam gives way to one line per win.
* **Check Before It Rolls** // The Loot Roll Sanity Check shows what Automated Rolls would do with random drops for your character.
