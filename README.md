# GogoLoot

Speedy auto loot empties corpses without the loot window, auto rolls Need, Greed, or Pass by quality, and master looting hands out drops for you. Clams, containers, and unlocked lockboxes open on their own. Loot faster, pull sooner.

**TL;DR:** You can't click corpses and pull the next pack at the same time. Don't let loot slow down your zug!

## Setup

1. Install the add-on, ideally using [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/gogoloot) or [Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/gogoloot).
2. Type `/gogo`, or Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button, to open the Options Interface.
3. Turn on Automated Rolls and pick how far it goes. It starts off, as a safety.
4. Raid leading? Turn on Automated Master Looting and pick who gets each quality.
5. _"If you ain't first, you're last."_

## Features

🥡 **Automated Opening** // Clams, crates, coin purses, and unlocked lockboxes open in your bags on their own. Lockbox tooltips show the Lockpicking skill each one needs.

🎲 **Automated Rolls** // Need, Greed, or Pass on everything up to the quality you pick, set apart for parties and raids. Give an item a roll of its own, or leave Intellect gear to you on your Warrior.

⚡ **Speedy Loot** // Corpses empty the moment you open them, with no loot window in the way. Hold Shift whenever you want a look.

💰 **Automated Master Looting** // Each drop goes to the player you picked for its quality the moment the loot window opens, and the raid hears who got what.

🦺 **Safety First** // Never rolls on recipes, books, mounts, pets, or legendaries, or on BoP gear unless you say so. Raid boss containers stay sealed for trading, and nothing opens in combat.

## Tips & Tricks

### Mini-Map Button

Hover for each feature's On/Off state, what Automated Rolls will roll in a party and in a raid, and any lockboxes still waiting on a Rogue.

| Click | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Left-Click | Toggle Automated Rolls |
| Right-Click | Toggle Automated Opening |
| Shift + Left-Click | Toggle Loot Announcements |
| Shift + Right-Click | Toggle Automated Master Looting |
| Shift + Middle-Click | Open the Options Interface |

### Auto Loot Setting

Speedy Loot and Automated Opening need the game's Auto Loot setting, so GogoLoot turns it on while either one is enabled and says so in chat when it does. Turn both off and the setting is left alone.

### Loot Toasts

New and off by default: turn on Loot Toasts to see each item and coin you loot on screen, plus your group's best drops. While they're on, your General chat tab's Item Loot and Money Loot lines step aside, and they come back when you turn the toasts off. Tell us what you think!

### Macros Created

Turn on Roll Macro and GogoLoot adds a `- Roll` macro to your General Macros: Left-Click rolls 1-100, Right-Click 1-99 for off-spec, and Middle-Click a raid roll for your group's size.
