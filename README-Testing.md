# GogoLoot // Test Plan

GogoLoot speeds up looting, rolls and opens for you, and hands out loot as master looter. This time we most want you to try the mini-map button's new clicks, Roll Audit, and the roll preview on item tooltips.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever and MoP Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Left-Click the mini-map button: the menu stays open until you Left-Click again or press Escape, and its blue **Toggle** rows turn each feature on and off.
2. Right-, Middle- or Shift-Click the mini-map button: nothing happens now.
3. With **Automated Rolls** on, hover an item in your bags and click one in chat: the tooltip ends with what GogoLoot would roll on it in a party and in a raid.
4. In a Group Loot dungeon, roll on a few drops, then open **Roll Audit** in the mini-map menu: **Recent Rolls** lists each item and its winner, and hovering one shows everyone's roll.
5. As master looter with players picked under **Loot Destinations**, log out, have the group disband while you're gone, and log back in: the picks are cleared.

## Everything Else

6. With **Enable Speedy Loot** on, loot a mob: everything lands in your bags with no loot window flashing up.
7. With your bags full, loot a mob: what won't fit stays in the loot window, and coin is still taken.
8. In a Group Loot party, gear your class wants keeps its roll window and the rest is greeded, as the **Loot Roll Sanity Check** page shows.
9. Loot a clam or other container: it opens by itself, but never in combat; on WoW Forever, try this in a fight.
10. On a Rogue, pick the lock on a lockbox: it opens by itself.
11. As master looter with **Enable Automated Master Looting** on, pick a player under **Loot Destinations**: the loot goes to them; on WoW Forever, try it mid-fight.
12. Type `/gogo`, then hover the mini-map button and click **GogoLoot Options**: both open GogoLoot in the game's Options window.
13. In combat, type `/gogo`: chat says the options can't open during combat.
