# GogoLoot // Test Plan

GogoLoot speeds up looting, rolls and opens for you, and hands out loot as master looter. This time we most want you to go fishing with Automated Opening on, and to watch what the mini-map menu says about it.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever and MoP Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. With a fishing pole equipped, fish up a clam: it stays shut and chat says why, until you turn on **With Fishing Pole Equipped** under **Automated Opening**.
2. Hover the mini-map button while fishing, or in a group with **Only While Solo** on: Automated Opening reads Paused and says why.
3. Carry a container the **Openables List** keeps on Ignore, then hover the mini-map button: it's listed under Automated Opening.
4. Hover a lockbox in the loot window and the trade window, and click a lockbox link in chat: each shows the Lockpicking skill it needs (not on MoP Classic).
5. In the mini-map menu, pick a roll from the Party or Raid flyout: the menu closes, and the flyout now links to **Loot Roll Sanity Check**.
6. On **Loot Toasts**, click **Unlock Position**, drag the toasts, then click **Lock Position** on the toasts' handle.

## Everything Else

7. With **Enable Speedy Loot** on, loot a mob: everything lands in your bags with no loot window flashing up.
8. With your bags full, loot a mob: what won't fit stays in the loot window, and coin is still taken.
9. In a Group Loot party, gear your class wants keeps its roll window and the rest is greeded, as the **Loot Roll Sanity Check** page shows.
10. Loot a clam or other container: it opens by itself, but never in combat; on WoW Forever, try this in a fight.
11. On a Rogue, pick the lock on a lockbox: it opens by itself.
12. As master looter with **Enable Automated Master Looting** on, pick a player under **Loot Destinations**: the loot goes to them; on WoW Forever, try it mid-fight.
13. Type `/gogo`, then hover the mini-map button and click **GogoLoot Options**: both open GogoLoot in the game's Options window.
14. In combat, type `/gogo`: chat says the options can't open during combat.
