# GogoLoot // Test Plan

GogoLoot speeds up looting, rolls and opens for you, and hands out loot as master looter. This time we most want you to try the new **Character Rules** page, since Automated Rolls now starts on for everyone.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever and MoP Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Log in after updating: chat says once that Automated Rolls is now on.
2. Open **Automated Rolls → Character Rules**: the armor, weapon types and stats your class uses start ticked, and **Reset to Class Defaults** puts back anything you change.
3. In a Group Loot party, gear your character wants keeps its roll window and the rest is greeded, just as **Sanity Check** at the bottom of Character Rules says.
4. While leveling, loot gear five or more levels below you: it's greeded, until you set **Automatically Greed on Low-Level Gear** to Off.
5. In a group, a Bind on Pickup item keeps its roll window until you turn on **Include Bind on Pickup** under Automated Rolls.
6. Set a clam to **Ignore** on the **Openables List**, then fish one up with **Enable Speedy Loot** on: it goes into your bags and stays shut.

## Everything Else

7. With **Enable Speedy Loot** on, loot a mob: everything lands in your bags with no loot window flashing up.
8. With your bags full, loot a mob: what won't fit stays in the loot window, and coin is still taken.
9. Loot a clam or other container: it opens by itself, but never in combat; on WoW Forever, try this in a fight.
10. On a Rogue, pick the lock on a lockbox: it opens by itself.
11. As master looter with **Enable Automated Master Looting** on, pick a player under **Loot Destinations**: the loot goes to them; on WoW Forever, try it mid-fight.
12. Trade a friend: they get one whisper listing everything that changed hands.
13. Type `/gogo`, then **Shift + Middle-Click** the mini-map button: both open GogoLoot in the game's Options window.
14. In combat, type `/gogo`: chat says the options can't open during combat.
